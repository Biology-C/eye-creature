## 最終修訂提示詞

Make a revised transparent pixel sprite atlas using the attached gray rat as reference. Exactly 4 columns by 2 rows, eight equally sized cells, no labels text ground shadows or backgrounds. Top row: same four right-facing gray rat poses neutral, scurry, hunched anticipation, lunge, but add very visible emerald GREEN CRYSTAL patches/shards embedded along back and shoulder of EVERY rat. No gore. Bottom row: four GREEN SLIME poses, rounded low jelly blob with two dark eyes, emerald body pale lime highlights; idle, squash, stretch, recover. This slime is what the rat becomes after first hit. Consistent logical pixel size, chunky square pixels, black outlines, limited palette. Each complete creature centered in its own cell with 15 percent empty transparent padding. All creatures same ground baseline within each cell. Preserve rat identity and style, no smooth antialiasing no glows. Genuine alpha transparency. These sprites will be sliced as a 4x2 grid.
