# Creature concepts V2

Tool: built-in image_gen. Pixel concept artwork; not animation assets.

## Initial concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Pixel art creature concept board for a whimsical exploration game for a six-year-old. Warm ivory background, crisp enlarged square pixels, compact readable silhouettes, charcoal outlines, limited muted palette matching an ivory eyeball hero with teal iris and dragon wings. Exactly four panels in 2x2 layout, ample whitespace, professional concept sheet not gameplay screenshot. Top left label '01 / CYAN BIRD': a flying CYAN bird creature, clear beak, two bird wings, short fan tail, light cyan belly and deeper turquoise feathers, mischievous not scary; one large flight pose and three smaller similar cyan birds suggesting multiplication. It must look avian, not a black ghost, not a bat. Top right label '02 / RED BEAR': a stocky RED BEAR chimera miniboss with round bear ears, broad muzzle, heavy bear paws, brick red fur, contrasting mismatched burgundy and coral body patches, subtle toy-like asymmetric seams, ivory face markings; no gore, no mechanical weapons. Beside it a small freestanding mirror showing a plain undistorted silhouette to suggest breaking an illusion, and a few inverted square map tile motifs, minimal. Bottom left label '03 / MOUSE HOUSE': a tiny charming mouse-scale timber house with round entrance, roof, a little mouse peeking out; neutral concept exterior, no shop icons, no healing icons, no enemy designation because function undecided. Bottom right label '04 / TREE CREATURE': a sentient tree with bark face, branch arms, rooted feet and leafy crown, expressive not horrifying; beside it a smaller fallen log/stump and two cyan birds taking flight illustrating cutting trees summons cyan birds. Keep trees visibly wood-and-leaf beings. No extra labels, no paragraph text. Characters coherent pixel density. Ground shadows are small hard pixel clusters. No soft gradients, no painterly textures, no gore, no horror. This is concept art, not animation sprites or final game assets.
